Must-Visit Places

Top Attractions in Seremban

Immerse yourself in nature, architecture, and local heritage spots that make Seremban unique.

Culture & View

Centipede Temple (Then Sze Koon)

Perched atop Wu Gong Hill, this historic Taoist temple features stunning panoramic views of Seremban town, peaceful koi ponds, and a giant centipede structure.

Open daily: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Nature

Seremban Lake Garden

One of Malaysia's oldest natural lake gardens. Beautifully manicured lawns, shady trees, and historic roots—including the site of the first rubber tree planted in the state.

Open 24 hours
Adventure

Gunung Angsi

Standing at 825 meters within Ulu Bendul Recreational Park, it offers an exhilarating 3-to-4-hour hiking trail through lush tropical rainforest streams.

Best visited in the morning
History

Negeri Sembilan State Museum

Showcasing traditional Minangkabau architecture (Rumah Gadang), featuring the historic Istana Ampang Tinggi palace built completely without iron nails.

Daily: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Heritage

Tuanku Ja'afar Royal Gallery

An architectural masterpiece dedicated to the life and reign of the late Yang di-Pertuan Besar of Negeri Sembilan, filled with royal artifacts and photographs.

Tue - Sun: 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM
Culture

Seremban Cultural Complex

A vibrant space celebrating local arts, traditional crafts, and cultural performances unique to the Minangkabau heritage of Negeri Sembilan.

Open daily for visitors

Culinary Delights

Famous Seremban Cravings

No trip to Seremban is complete without trying these legendary local delicacies.

Seremban Beef Noodles

A legendary dish featuring smooth rice noodles topped with tender beef slices, rich aromatic dark gravy, crushed peanuts, pickled salted vegetables, and savory minced meat.

Top Spot: Seremban Pasar Besar Must Try

Seremban Siew Pau

Famous for its flaky, golden-brown pastry crust stuffed with savory, slightly sweet barbecued pork (char siu) filling. Baked fresh daily by local heritage bakeries.

Top Spot: Kee Mei Siew Pau Souvenir Favorite

Refreshing Cendol

Beat the tropical Malaysian heat with a bowl of shaved ice drizzled with rich coconut milk, aromatic Gula Melaka (palm sugar syrup), green rice flour jelly, and red beans.

Top Spot: Local Street Stalls Refreshing

Plan Your Trip

How to Get Here & Travel Tips

Quick practical guide to ensure a smooth journey to Seremban.

By Train (KTM Komuter)

Take the KTM Komuter train directly from KL Sentral or Sepang/Nilai down to Seremban Railway Station. It's affordable, comfortable, and drops you right near the town center.

By Car / Highway

A convenient 45-to-60-minute drive from Kuala Lumpur via the North-South Expressway (PLUS). Perfect for a spontaneous weekend road trip with friends or family.

Best Time to Visit

Early mornings are ideal for visiting Gunung Angsi or wandering around the vibrant Seremban Pasar Besar wet market before food stalls get busy around lunchtime.
